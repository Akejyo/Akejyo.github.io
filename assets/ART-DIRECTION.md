# Arctic folklore layer

The landscape is original generated artwork. The weaving details are original SVG geometry: open diamonds, offset triangles, chevrons, and repeating lines. They do not reproduce the supplied emblems and are not presented as authentic Sámi cultural symbols.

## Landscape

- Final asset: `assets/arctic-frontier.png`.
- Method: built-in image-generation tool, not CLI/API fallback.
- Role: static home hero background, without a character.
- Layout: original typography and composition remain in HTML. A pale CSS scrim preserves contrast behind the words at every breakpoint.

### Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: static editorial website hero background, wide 2:1 panoramic image. Create an ORIGINAL atmospheric arctic wilderness in natural overcast winter daylight: immense open snowfield in foreground, far-away low mountain ranges softened by cold atmospheric haze, a dark conifer forest along the distant right-hand valley edge. Landscape must feel vast, quiet, contemplative, slightly melancholy. The left 55 percent must be luminous low-contrast pale snow and mist with very large clean negative space so existing dark navy editorial text can be placed there. More natural detailed forest and mountain texture in the right third; layered distance rather than dramatic looming cliffs. Restrained snow white, cool blue-grey, deep muted navy. Fine natural snow texture, soft painterly photographic finish, beautiful environmental detail at the right without visual clutter. No person in this image. No structures, no supernatural phenomena, no glowing objects, no magic, no portals, no particles in air, no typography, no logo, no symbol, no watermark. Not game concept art. Original composition; no recreation of any attached composition. Output one finished landscape image.

## Ornament and material rules

- `src/folklore.js`: original `TextileBand` and normal left/right `FootprintTrail` SVG components; all decorative SVGs have `aria-hidden="true"` and `focusable="false"`.
- `src/folklore.css`: an additive layer, without type-size, reading-width, route, or structural layout changes.
- Paper grain: 1.8%; weave: 3%; snow grain: 2.5%. These are static background layers with no pointer events.
- Weaving appears only at the current-expedition separator, existing About divider, and a short footer stitch.
- BrokenDiamond accents are restricted to the active navigation link, section labels, article metadata, and the existing marks.
- One ordinary pair of footprints appears in the footer. No movement, anomalous ordering, or missing toes.
- The original vector landscape studies remain in their MDX essay because its prose explicitly describes those vector images.
